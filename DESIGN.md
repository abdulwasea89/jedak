# Design rules

The site is an engineering index, not a marketing landing page. Use the persistent rail and actual shared grid boundaries as the structure. Keep the background near-black and reserve high contrast for readable text and links. No glow, gradient, fake terminal, floating cards, decorative chips, or manufactured metrics. Use a compact monogram and restrained system typography. Preserve mobile navigation and meaningful focus states. Animation is limited to short entry and scroll reveals, with reduced-motion support and no artificial scroll delay. Technical writing should state a concrete problem, assumptions, implementation constraints, failure cases and sources. No invented experiments, client outcomes, metrics or personal anecdotes. Drafts must be reviewed before attributing them to the site owner.
